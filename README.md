# Tableau-Data-Science-Job-Salaries
**Data Science Job Salaries Dashboard – Project Explanation**

This Tableau dashboard analyzes global Data Science salaries across job titles, experience levels, employment types, company sizes, and countries.

The dashboard shows that **Full-Time employment accounts for nearly 97% of all jobs**, making it the dominant hiring model. **Senior professionals represent 46% of the workforce** and earn substantially higher salaries than entry-level employees.

Among job roles, **Applied Data Scientist** and **Applied Machine Learning Scientist** receive the highest salaries, while **AI Scientist** and **Analytics Engineer** also command premium compensation. The analysis also shows that **Contract Expert** professionals earn the highest salaries overall.

From a geographic perspective, the **United States** has both the largest number of employees and the highest average salaries, while countries like India contribute a growing share of the talent pool but at lower salary levels.

The key business takeaway is that salary is primarily influenced by **experience, specialization, company size, employment type, and location**. This dashboard helps job seekers understand salary trends and helps companies benchmark compensation across roles and regions.
